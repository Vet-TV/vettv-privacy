### Privacy Policy — Effective September 10, 2026

**What We Collect:**
Lemonade Tycoon - WebUI Edition and all Vet GamingTV titles do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data, location data, or user content from your device. Games run locally in WebView2/WebUI. No account required. No ads SDK that collects personal data. No analytics that identifies you.

Website vet-tv.github.io/vettv-privacy/ is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs (IP, user-agent) for hosting purposes per GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add additional trackers.

**What We Do NOT Collect:**
- No name, email, address, phone without you emailing us at vetgaming1982@outlook.com
- No precise location, health, financial data
- No children’s data — our games are suitable for all ages but we do not knowingly collect from children under 13

**Third-Party Services:**
Microsoft Store, Steam, Google Play, Epic Games Store may collect purchase and device info per their own policies. Twitch Drops (Ubisoft, Outright Games) are handled by Twitch/Ubisoft/Outright per their policies. Boosteroid Cloud Gaming is handled per Boosteroid policy when you use their service.

**Contact & Requests:**
For privacy questions, data requests, or support: **vetgaming1982@outlook.com**. We will respond within 30 days.
