Privacy Policy

Vet GamingTV (VetTV) — Universal privacy policy for all games and applications developed by Vet GamingTV.
Effective: September 10, 2026 • Last Updated: September 10, 2026
Applies to all current and future titles including Lemonade Tycoon - WebUI Edition
Microsoft Store • Policy 10.5.1 Compliant Steam Store Google Play Store Epic Games Store
Quick summary: Our games are designed to be private by default. We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal information. Game progress (money, levels, saves) is stored locally on your device. We do not use advertising SDKs, cross-app trackers, or analytics that identify you. Any data collection you see in a store (like purchase history) is handled by the store itself — Steam, Microsoft, Google, or Epic — under their own privacy policies, not ours.

1. Scope

This policy applies to all software, games, and apps created by Vet GamingTV ("we", "us", "our"), including but not limited to desktop games, WebUI editions, mobile apps, and prototypes distributed via:

  • Microsoft Store (including Xbox / Windows apps)
  • Steam (Valve Corporation)
  • Google Play Store (Android)
  • Epic Games Store
  • Direct downloads from our GitHub Pages or official website

Product-specific details: Lemonade Tycoon - WebUI Edition (Microsoft Store ID: 9MZJ8R55XLW2) is covered by this policy.

If a specific game includes online multiplayer, cloud saves, or leaderboards in the future, we will add a supplemental notice inside that game. The base policy remains no personal data collection.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. Across all our titles:

  • No name, email, physical address, phone number
  • No precise or approximate location
  • No photos, videos, contacts, or files outside the game
  • No advertising ID (IDFA, GAID), no cross-app tracking
  • No voice, facial recognition, or biometric data
  • No third-party analytics that identify you (no Firebase Analytics, no Facebook SDK, etc.)

What IS processed locally: Gameplay state such as in-game currency, inventory, level, day number, settings, and high scores. This is saved via local storage, localStorage / IndexedDB / WebView2 / platform save files. It never leaves your device unless you manually export or use a platform-provided cloud save feature.

3. Platform-Specific Data (Not Collected By Us)

When you download our games from a store, that store operator may independently collect data under its own policy. We do not receive or control that data beyond what is necessary to provide the game.

Microsoft Store Microsoft may collect purchase history, device info, and crash diagnostics per Microsoft Privacy Statement. Requirement: 10.5.1 & 10.6
Steam Store Valve may collect Steam ID, purchase info, playtime, crash reports per Steam Privacy Policy. We use no additional Steamworks personal data beyond achievement unlocks if applicable.
Google Play Store Google may collect device info, installs, and purchases per Google Privacy Policy. Android permissions: Our games request no dangerous permissions (no camera, location, contacts). If a game requests storage permission, it's only for local saves.
Epic Games Store Epic may collect Epic Account ID, purchase history per Epic Privacy Policy. We do not embed Epic tracking beyond store distribution.

4. How We Use Information

Because we collect no personal information, we have no use for marketing, profiling, selling, or advertising. Local save data is used solely to restore your progress when you reopen the game.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or transmit personal information to third parties. We do not include third-party ad networks or data brokers in our games.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Windows / Microsoft Store: Saved via app local folder and WebView2 storage.
  • Steam: Saved via Steam Cloud (if enabled by user) or local save directory. Controlled by Steam.
  • Google Play / Android: Saved via app private storage. Uninstalling deletes it unless you use Google Play Games cloud save.
  • Epic: Saved via local Epic save directory.

Uninstalling or using "Reset Progress" in-game will delete local data. We use reasonable technical measures to ensure our games do not transmit personal data.

7. Children's Privacy

Our games are family-friendly and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable). If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at support@vetgamingtv.com and we will delete it. This complies with COPPA, Google Play Families Policy, and Microsoft Store child safety requirements.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Since we store no personal data on our servers, there is no account to access or delete with us. You control all local data:

  • Delete saves via in-game reset
  • Clear app storage: Windows Settings > Apps > Storage, Android Settings > Apps > Storage > Clear Data
  • Opt out of platform cloud saves via Steam / Google Play / Microsoft / Epic settings

For data collected by the stores themselves, please use their respective privacy dashboards.

9. Data Retention

Local save data is retained until you delete it. We retain no server-side personal data, so there is no server retention period. Support emails are retained only to respond and then archived for up to 12 months.

10. International Users

Our games operate offline and do not transfer personal data internationally. If you contact us by email, your message may be processed in the United States where we are based.

11. Third-Party Links and Hosting

Our games contain no external links except optional links to our support or this privacy policy. This privacy policy itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may collect anonymized server logs (IP address) per GitHub Privacy Statement.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new games, platform requirements, or legal requirements. We will update the "Last Updated" date and keep the same URL: https://vet-tv.github.io/vettv-privacy/ so all store listings remain compliant. Material changes will be noted here.
